Habitfern

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version
  • Your habits stay on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts.
  • We don't collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

This policy explains how the Habitfern mobile app ("the app"), published by Fernleaf Labs ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • The habits you create (name, icon, color, schedule, reminder time and order)
  • Your daily check-ins and streak history
  • Your app settings, such as theme and accent color
  • Whether you have unlocked Habitfern Pro

This data never leaves your device through the app, and we can't access it. We don't require an account, an email address or any other personal details.

Device backups

Android's built-in backup feature may include the app's data in your device backup, stored in your Google account and governed by Google's Privacy Policy. This lets you restore your habits on a new phone. You can turn off backups in your device's system settings. We have no access to these backups.

Backup files

If you use "Back up habits", the app creates a file with your habits and history and saves it wherever you choose, for example your phone's storage or a cloud drive. The file is handled by the storage service you pick, and we never receive a copy. "Restore from backup" only reads the file you select.

Rating prompt

Once you reach a streak milestone, the app may show Google Play's built-in rating prompt. Google handles this prompt and any rating you give, and the app is not told whether or how you rated it.

Purchases

Habitfern Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your card number, name or billing address. The app only asks Google Play whether your Pro purchase is active.

Notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. If you allow notifications, the app also restores your reminders after your phone restarts. No reminder data is sent anywhere. You can turn off a habit's reminder in the app, or turn off all notifications in your device settings, at any time.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We don't build profiles or follow your activity across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual habits in the app. To erase all app data, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Since we hold no copy of your data, there's nothing more for us to delete. Contact us anyway if you have questions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
